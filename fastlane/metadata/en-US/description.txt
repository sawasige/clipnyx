Clipnyx is a clipboard manager that lives in your macOS menu bar. It automatically records your copy history for instant recall via a hotkey, lets you organize frequently used text into snippets and folders, and even recognizes text inside images and PDFs with on-device OCR — so you can search and reuse it.

It automatically records copied text, images, URLs, source code, colors, and more — ready to recall whenever you need them.

KEY FEATURES
- Instantly open the history panel with a hotkey (⌘⇧V)
- Quickly pick items with number keys (1–9)
- Auto-categorizes 11 content types: text, images, PDF, URLs, HTML, CSV, source code, and more
- Category filters and text search to quickly find what you need
- On-device OCR for images and PDFs — search your screenshots by their contents or pull the text out (runs locally, nothing sent externally)
- Organize items with favorites, folders, and a Collection view
- Save reusable snippets and templates to paste again and again
- Convert rich text, URLs, and recognized image text to plain text
- Thumbnail previews for images and PDFs
- Store up to 500 history items (choose from 20/50/100/200/500)
- Customize which categories to record
- Fully customizable hotkey
- Launch at login support
- History is stored locally. Nothing is ever sent externally.
