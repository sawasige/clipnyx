Clipnyx is a menu bar clipboard history manager.

Basic usage:
1. Launch the app — it appears in the menu bar. No permission dialog is shown at
   launch or at any other time.
2. Open the history panel from the menu bar icon or with the global hot key
   (default ⌘+Shift+V).
3. Selecting an item copies its content to the clipboard. Paste it into any app
   with ⌘V.

Other features:
- Favorites, folders, and a Collection view for organizing saved items.
- Convert rich text, URLs, and more to plain text.
- Per-category filters to choose what gets recorded.

The app does not request any system permissions and shows no permission prompts.
All clipboard history is stored locally on the device; nothing is sent to any
external server. Every feature can be tested without granting anything.
